FIELD MANUAL

How to Play!

Goal: Survive for 36 months without going bankrupt or dropping below 0% reputation!

1. Action Points (AP) & Budget

Every month you receive AP and must pay Baseline Ops Costs. Spend your AP and Budget to invest in projects. When you are done, click "Advance Month".

2. The NIST RMF Pillars

To safely deploy AI, you must invest in four governance pillars. If a critical incident occurs, your defense is calculated based on these pillars.

  • MAP: Contextualize risks (Defends against Edge Cases)
  • MEASURE: Test and evaluate (Defends against Bias)
  • MANAGE: Prioritize and mitigate (Defends against Hallucinations/Security)
  • GOVERN: Cultivate culture (Defends against Compliance Audits)

3. Deployment & Revenue

Once a project's Dev progress hits 100%, you can Deploy it to generate monthly revenue. But beware: deployed projects have a 40% chance every month to trigger Critical Incidents!

4. Trustworthy AI

If you push all 4 NIST pillars (Map, Measure, Manage, Govern) to 100% on a project, it achieves Trustworthy AI Certification. It will double its revenue and become immune to incidents.
